03 — The Artist

About Sheeba

Painter of remembered places.

— from the studio, Kerala

"This piece is part of my Glimpses of Kerala series — painted from a memory, a scent, a silence."

Sheeba Manghat Narayanan paints the Kerala she carries with her — the temple tree she sat under as a child holding her grandmother's hand, the fishermen who row because they have to and love because they choose to, the bull races where tradition runs through mud and becomes memory.

She works in oil on canvas, one original at a time. The paintings began as a single series, Glimpses of Kerala, and have grown into a quiet record of a coast, its mornings, and its people.

Practice
