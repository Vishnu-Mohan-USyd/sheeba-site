## Notes & caveats

- **Clean, text-free artwork = folder 1.** The Instagram reels all have the
  artist's caption text overlaid on the video, so extracted frames contain text.
  The frames are still useful as quick references, but the Drive DSLR photos in
  folder 1 are the true high-resolution, text-free images of each painting.
- **Carousel post (DMVmv7JzKWW):** the share link pointed at slide 5, but the
  public/anonymous fetch returned 3 video clips (all of the green-landscape work).
  If there were extra image-only slides, fetching them would require Instagram
  login — say the word and I can grab them.
- **Duplicates removed:** 10 byte-identical duplicate files (mostly in 'Sold Works')
  were de-duplicated during organising.
- **'Testimonials' and '_Press, Media & Archive'** are not paintings (client
  testimonials, a TV episode .mpg, newspaper article, Photosynth pages, a DVD rip).
