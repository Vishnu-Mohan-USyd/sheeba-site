Survival

--- Caption ---

A song of survival.
Of sweat, sun, and soaked prayers.
Of people who row because they have to…
and love because they choose to.
This painting is for them.
This piece is part 
of my *Glimpses of Kerala* series — 
painted from a memory, a scent, 
a silence.
