--- Caption ---

Artists see stories where others see storms.

This one — Village Competition III — came alive when I tried to capture the heartbeat between the splash, the soil, and the strength of the man behind it.

It’s strange how the loudest moments often end up whispering the most.

What’s one memory that feels loud — but peaceful — every time you think of it?
