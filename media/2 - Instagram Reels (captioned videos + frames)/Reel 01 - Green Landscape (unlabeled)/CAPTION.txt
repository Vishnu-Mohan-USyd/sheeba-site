--- Caption ---

Yes, I had kept leaves like this in my books…
almost forgotten — until one fell out again,
bringing with it an old, familiar bliss. 🍃

This is the very tree that stood in the temple compound
where I went almost every day,
holding my grandmother’s hand.

It’s where I sat in quiet thought…
where I played on hot afternoons.
It watched me grow. It guided me.
It still stands there today —
and even though I’m far away,
I still hear its leaves rustle in the breeze
and feel that same wind on my face.

If this stirs something in you…
tell me what it reminds you of.
